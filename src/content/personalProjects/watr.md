Watr helps you make time for a water break: get up, get a glass, and take a sip. Set your routine and choose where reminders should run, with a separate alert mode for each place.

The Android MVP is built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, with support for English and Spanish.

### Features

- Scheduled hydration breaks with native Android alarms and reminders.
- Sound or vibration-only alerts, including separate settings for home and the office.
- Optional location profiles that keep reminders quiet outside your saved places.
- A two-step camera flow: show your container, then perform a gesture consistent with taking a sip.
- Local history of completed, skipped, and missed breaks.
- Monthly and annual subscriptions through Google Play and RevenueCat.

Camera images are processed on the phone without recording or uploading them. Visual verification is experimental: it recognizes containers and a drinking gesture, not the liquid's composition, swallowing, or a volume consumed.
